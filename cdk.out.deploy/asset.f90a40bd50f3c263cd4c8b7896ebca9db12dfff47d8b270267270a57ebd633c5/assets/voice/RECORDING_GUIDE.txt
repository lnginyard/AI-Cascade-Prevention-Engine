VOICE RECORDING GUIDE — Cascade Prevention Engine Demo
=======================================================

Drop your personal MP3 recordings into this folder.
File names must match exactly. The app automatically uses
your recordings instead of the browser's TTS voice.

Recommended recording tips:
  - Use your phone's Voice Memo app (exports M4A → rename to .mp3)
    OR use QuickTime > File > New Audio Recording on Mac.
  - Quiet room, normal speaking pace.
  - Say the line naturally, then trim silence from start/end.
  - Export / save as MP3 (or rename .m4a → .mp3 — modern browsers
    play both, just name the file with .mp3 extension).
