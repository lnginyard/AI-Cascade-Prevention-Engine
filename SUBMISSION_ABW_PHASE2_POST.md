## What I Built

I built the **AI Cascade Prevention Engine**, a simulation and operations platform designed to detect and reduce cascading failures before they become major incidents.

I also released a companion article that explains the app and development journey at an executive level, while still breaking down the specific technical decisions that matter.

I designed both the app and the article experience to feel professional, seamless, sleek, and easy to operate.

For this competition, I chose the **Social Good track**.

I chose Social Good because this project is about reducing outage impact, improving response speed, and helping teams protect the people and communities who depend on digital services.

Core capabilities today:

- Ingests telemetry from operational signals
- Detects anomaly patterns early
- Predicts blast radius across dependencies
- Suggests and orchestrates remediation actions
- Gives operators a clear interface for decisions and visibility
- Scales from team-level testing to enterprise-level operational governance

## What It Can Potentially Become

As I continued building, I realized some parts of this app introduced patterns I had not seen packaged this way before in this workflow, and some components may have **patent potential**.

That realization pushed me to take the project further and equip it with additional services and higher-level architecture so it could grow beyond a single prototype.

Potential next capabilities:

- More advanced policy-aware remediation guardrails
- Stronger historical forecasting and confidence calibration
- Expanded cross-account and multi-region coordination
- Deeper simulation modes for pre-incident planning and training
- Broader enterprise integrations for governance and operations
